# UI/UX Growth Recommendations

Date: 2026-06-09

## Conversion

- Give each live product a concrete action: open app, view demo, join waitlist, or contact sales.
- Avoid placeholder links on public cards.
- Add event tracking to every primary CTA.

## Product Trust

- Show what each product actually does with screenshots, short workflow examples, or demo links.
- Make live versus coming-soon status visually distinct and behaviorally different.
- Use support/contact copy that sets expectations for response time and support channel.

## Mobile

- Verify the floating email button and back-to-top button do not cover form controls or footer links.
- Keep product-card tap targets large and predictable.
- Check long product names and taglines against small viewport widths.

## Content

- Treat roadmap and changelog content as public commitments.
- Keep copy specific to WKL Labs products instead of generic SaaS language.
- Add a lightweight editorial rule: every roadmap or changelog item should have a date, status, and product owner.
