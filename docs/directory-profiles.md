## Portfolio entries

### 1. Nabbee: booking and deposits for service businesses

- **Category:** Mobile App Development, Custom Software Development
- **Industry:** Beauty and wellness services
- **Link:** https://wkllabs.com/case-studies/nabbee
- **Image:** `public/case-studies/nabbee-app.webp`

> Nabbee is a booking platform for salons, spas, and other appointment-based businesses in the United States and the Philippines. We researched how these businesses manage bookings, then designed, built, and launched the product on the web, iOS, and Android.
>
> The platform gives each business a branded booking page, optional deposits (card payments through Stripe in the US; GCash, Maya, or bank transfer in the Philippines), an owner dashboard, a walk-in queue, and email and app reminders. Key engineering work included preventing double bookings, supporting two currencies and time zones, and production monitoring with automated alerts.
>
> Built with Next.js, React Native (Expo), PostgreSQL, and Stripe.

### 2. Spell Collector: trading card marketplace

- **Category:** Web Development
- **Industry:** E-commerce and marketplaces
- **Link:** https://spellcollector.com
- **Image:** `public/products/spellcollector.webp`

> Spell Collector is a marketplace where Magic: The Gathering and Pokémon collectors compare prices, buy from local shops, and manage their collections. We designed and built the platform with Next.js, TypeScript, and PostgreSQL, including shop listings, collection tracking, and an administration area.

### 3. Property performance dashboard (client project)

- **Category:** Business Intelligence
